Synthetic equation example:

An inline parameter \(c\) and a first matrix:

\[ \begin{bmatrix}2 & c & 0\\0 & 3 & 1\\0 & 0 & 4\end{bmatrix}. \]

An elementary operation with another matrix:

\[ R_2\leftarrow R_2+R_1 \quad \begin{bmatrix}2 & c & 0\\2 & c+3 & 1\\0 & 0 & 4\end{bmatrix}. \]

A generic inline expression \(c+3\) and the scalar \(2\):

\[ c+3=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{c=-3}. \]

A third matrix:

\[ \begin{bmatrix}2 & -3 & 0\\0 & 3 & 1\\0 & 0 & 4\end{bmatrix}. \]
